## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an immersive, theater-grade digital sanctuary engineered for cinephiles and discerning modern streamers. It targets an audience that values effortless discovery, high-fidelity presentation, and a fluid, non-distracting viewing environment. 

The emotional signature is high-energy yet refined—combining the electric vibrancy of neon cinema marquees with the quiet sophistication of an OLED-optimized viewing parlor.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** and **Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-deep obsidian surfaces let multi-format artwork and trailer previews command focal attention without UI glare.
- Translucent frosted glass overlays provide contextual secondary controls, volume adjustments, and metadata panels without interrupting screen immersion.
- Intense energetic orange anchors all interactive drive points (playback, conversions, primary scrubbing), while electric violet delivers curated prestige, category categorization, and loyalty tiers.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly within a deep cinematic hierarchy, optimized for low-light room viewing and high dynamic range display panels.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0C10`):** The foundational substrate representing complete visual silence.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#12141C`):** Rails, carousels, and persistent navigation backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1B1E2B`):** Interactive card backings, active modals, and drawer components.
- **Structural Outlines (`#2A2E3D`):** Ghost borders maintaining structural clarity between dense media assets.

### Accent Mechanics
- **Primary Playback Orange (`#FF6800` / `#FF7700`):** Reserved for playback trigger points, resume badges, watch progress fills, and critical purchase actions.
- **Electric Violet (`#6C00D6` / `#7928CA`):** Deployed for premium status, exclusive premiere indicators, curated collection badges, and audio format attributes (Dolby Atmos, 4K HDR).
- **Foreground Spectrum:** Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, Off-White Slate (`#E2E4E9`) for body content, and Subdued Steel (`#8E94A4`) for timestamps, specs, and secondary metadata.

## Typography

The type system balances commanding editorial titles with crisp, high-density metadata legibility.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings geometry, authority, and warmth to marquee show titles, hero banners, and carousel categories. Tight negative tracking across display scales delivers a polished cinematic poster appearance.
- **Inter** handles narrative synopses, episode logs, time codes, and media specifications. Its neutral grotesque cadence prevents eye fatigue and ensures effortless legibility across diverse screen viewing distances (from mobile handhelds to 10-foot television interfaces).
- **Label scaling** uses uppercase casing with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for technical badges (4K, HDR, CC, 5.1, TV-MA).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an edge-to-edge fluid horizontal rhythm with strict vertical modular grouping.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a 12-column dynamic grid with a default outer canvas margin of `3.5rem` and gutters of `1.5rem`. Continuous horizontal shelf carousels bypass right-hand margin truncation with fading edge masks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Transitions to an 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Hero title cards transition from multi-column split views to layered vertical stacks.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Operates on a 4-column structure with `1.25rem` safe-area canvas margins and compact `0.75rem` card gutters, maximizing poster real estate.

Vertical stacking between rows respects an 8-point base rhythm, giving hero modules `space-xl` breathing room, while content tiers and episodic lists standardise on `space-md` separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle tonal layering combined with frosted glassmorphism rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Pure `#0B0C10` background absorbing stray light.
- **Surface Rails (Level 1):** Flat `#12141C` backplates with hairline outlines (`1px solid #2A2E3D`).
- **Glass Overlay (Level 2):** Frosted glass modals, top navigation bars, and floating video player overlays use `rgba(18, 20, 28, 0.75)` with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur and an inner edge highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Lift & Focus (Level 3):** Hovered or remote-selected media cards lift with a `scale(1.04)` transition, projecting a directional ambient glow tinted with the primary or secondary tone: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(255, 104, 0, 0.25)` for live/featured media, and `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(108, 0, 214, 0.35)` for original content.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, rounded architectural standard (`roundedness: 2`).

- **Standard Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for technical metadata badges, mini chips, text input boxes, and progress tracks.
- **Cards & Modals (`1rem` / `16px`):** Primary movie and TV poster cards, episodic panels, and dropdown menus utilize medium-large rounding to soften high-contrast poster artwork.
- **Promotional Banners & Dialogs (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Featured billboard takeovers, detail popovers, and floating playback controls leverage extra-large corner radii.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Playback triggers, live indicator chips, and audio filter tags employ full circular caps (`9999px`) to immediately separate interaction verbs from square content artwork.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Playback Button:** Pill-shaped, high-energy `#FF6800` background with pure white typography and icons. Hover state increases brightness to `#FF7700` accompanied by a soft orange glow.
- **Secondary Watchlist / More Info:** Pill-shaped, frosted dark glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border and white text.
- **Tertiary / Icon Buttons:** Circular 40px or 48px glass buttons for mute, volume, episode select, and audio settings.

### Media Poster Cards
- **Aspect Ratios:** Vertical portrait (2:3) for cinematic films, landscape (16:9) for series episodes and live broadcasts.
- **Structure:** `1rem` corner rounding, deep `#1B1E2B` placeholder base, and subtle `1px solid #2A2E3D` boundary.
- **Hover & Focus:** Smooth 250ms ease-out scale expansion, elevating above sibling cards, with an auto-revealed preview panel showcasing trailer snippets, quick play buttons, and matching percentage chips.

### Badges & Chips
- **Quality & Audio Specs (4K, HDR, Dolby Atmos):** Compact rectangular badges with `0.25rem` corners, semi-translucent slate backgrounds (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), subtle white border, and bold uppercase text (`label-sm`).
- **Originals & Premium Accent Badges:** Electric violet gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #6C00D6, #7928CA)`) with crisp white lettering.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped tags defaulting to transparent surfaces with `#2A2E3D` borders, shifting to solid white text and `#FF6800` border accents when active.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Elongated pill or `0.75rem` rounded input with `#12141C` background, leading search icon, embedded shortcut indicator, and a transition to `#2A2E3D` border focus with violet focus-ring illumination.

### Video Player Scrub Bar & HUD
- **Scrub Bar:** 4px slimline bar expanding to 8px on hover/scrub. Unwatched background in `#2A2E3D`, buffer caching represented in `#8E94A4`, and active playback elapsed in intense brand `#FF6800` with an animated pulse scrub handle.
